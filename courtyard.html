The Courtyard Bar & Restaurant.

A proper café and restaurant at the heart of the village, open to everyone and not just the businesses on site. Nominated for the Good Food Guide.

Eat & drink

Breakfast, lunch, and somewhere to think.

The Courtyard sits next to Barn 4 and Barn 7, with a covered walkway from The Loft conference room. For the businesses on site it's the meeting room that isn't a meeting room, somewhere to take a client, interview a candidate, or get away from the desk for an hour.

The menu runs from brunch through to burgers, pizza, tapas and Sunday dining, and you can order ahead online for collection if you'd rather eat at your desk or take it home.
