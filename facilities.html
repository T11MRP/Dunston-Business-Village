Connectivity & IT

The infrastructure is run in-house rather than outsourced, so support is on site rather than on a ticket queue somewhere else.

  • Gigabit leased lines
  • Fully networked offices
  • On-site IT support team
  • VPN and PBX telephony
  • Raised computer flooring
  • Full disaster recovery solutions
  • Video conferencing suite

Access & security

The site is open around the clock, with entry controlled at both the gate and the door.

  • 24/7 access, 365 days a year
  • Door access control systems
  • Door intercom to reception
  • CCTV with number plate recognition
  • Covered, secure parking
  • Staffed reception at Woodland Lodge

Meeting & conference rooms

Three rooms, from one-to-ones to 40+.

All three have free WiFi and a catering service available. Bookable by tenants and, subject to availability, by visiting businesses.

  • Free WiFi
  • Catering service available

Up to 8 people

Meeting Room 19

  • Larger meeting room for small teams
  • Free WiFi and an 84″ digital screen
  • Catering service available

40+ delegates

Recognition

A restoration project, not a new build.

Dunston Business Village was created by restoring eight derelict 19th-century farm buildings rather than clearing the site. That approach is what the awards below recognised, and it's why the place looks and feels the way it does.

  • 2008Business Contribution to Regeneration, South Staffordshire
  • 2010Second place, Regeneration Awards; second place, RegenWM
  • 2011Winner, RICS Best Regeneration Project, West Midlands. Also a UK shortlisted finalist
  • 2012Recognised with a Royal Patronage visit
