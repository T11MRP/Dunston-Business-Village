The buildings

Barns, lodges and log cabins.

The heart of the site is eight character buildings dating from the 1800s, restored rather than replaced, which is why the layouts vary so much from one to the next. Alongside them sits a growing village of timber log cabins, each with its own door number and, in several cases, its own name: Owls Hoot, Hedgehog Hollow, Butterfly House, The Rabbit Hole.

Between them they cover most requirements: a single room for a sole practitioner, a split suite for a growing team, two floors of cellular offices, or a large open-plan floor plate with a mezzanine above.

Two of the barns are given over to covered, secure parking, and there are three car parks across the site.
