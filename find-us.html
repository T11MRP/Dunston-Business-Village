Parking & permits

Parking is free, but you need a permit.

There are three car parks on site with dedicated visitor and disabled spaces. Parking doesn't cost anything, but it is permit-controlled and enforced by number plate recognition, so every vehicle needs to be registered.

◆

Visiting for the day

Scan the QR code displayed by the business you're visiting to apply for a free parking pass valid for up to 24 hours.

  • Free, and available on the day
  • Each building has its own QR code, so ask your host
  • Must be completed within 4 hours of entering the site
◆

Visiting regularly

If you're in and out over a period, apply for a temporary visitor permit covering 7 or 30 days.

  • Apply at dunstonbusinessvillage.com/tempparking
  • Requires 48 working hours' notice
  • Not available same-day
◆

Working here

Staff based on site apply for a permanent permit, which renews every 12 months.

  • Apply at dunstonbusinessvillage.com/parkingpermit
  • Requires 48 working hours' notice
  • Not available same-day

Worth knowing before you arrive

Permits are issued at the point of acceptance and entry on the system and cannot be back-dated, even on the same day. They are non-transferable and linked to the vehicle registration number rather than to a person or a company. Permit issuance is subject to a fair use policy on a per-company basis, and permits are reviewed in regular scheduled audits.
